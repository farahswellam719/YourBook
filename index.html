Help & troubleshooting

Getting started

  1. Drag a PDF into the reader page or click "Choose a PDF" to upload. The book is saved to your browser only — it never leaves your device.
  2. Wait for processing. Long books (500+ pages) take 20-40 seconds.
  3. Press Play. The "Robotic" voice is free and uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis. The "Natural" voice uses ElevenLabs and sounds much better, but requires a free account.
  4. Click the Chapters button to jump to a specific section.

Highlighting and notes

Two ways to highlight passages:

  • Drag-select any text in the reader panel. A golden "Highlight" tooltip appears above your selection — click it to open the highlight dialog with that exact passage.
  • Click the gold "Highlight" button in the player controls. This captures roughly 30 words around the current playback position.

In the dialog, pick a color (yellow, blue, green, pink, orange, or purple), optionally write a note and add tags, then save. Highlights stay visible in the reader so you can see your annotations as you listen.

All your highlights and notes appear on the Notes tab, grouped by book and chapter. Click any note to jump back to that exact passage in the reader.

ElevenLabs API key setup

The natural voice uses ElevenLabs. Here's how to set it up properly:

  1. Go to elevenlabs.io and sign up for a free account. The free tier gives you 10,000 characters per month — about 75 minutes of audio.
  2. In your ElevenLabs dashboard, go to Profile → API Keys and click "Create API Key."
  3. Important: enable these permissions when creating the key:
    • text_to_speech — required to generate audio
    • voices_read — required to list your voices in the dropdown
    • models_read — recommended
    If voices_read is missing, the audio will play but the dropdown will be empty.
  4. Copy the entire key — it usually starts with sk_ and is around 50 characters long.
  5. Paste it into YourBook. The app will strip any whitespace automatically.

API error codes explained
401
Invalid key

The API key was rejected. Most common causes:

  • Hidden whitespace or invisible characters when copy-pasting (especially on mobile). The app strips these automatically, but check the character count next to "Key saved" — ElevenLabs keys are usually 50+ chars.
  • The key was revoked or deleted on ElevenLabs.
  • You pasted a key from a different account.
403
Permission denied

The key works but lacks permission to read voices. On ElevenLabs, edit the key and enable voices_read and text_to_speech permissions, or create a new key with full permissions.

429
Rate limited

You've hit ElevenLabs' rate limit. Wait 30 seconds and try again. The free tier allows about 2 concurrent requests at most.

402
Out of credits

Your monthly character quota is used up. Upgrade your ElevenLabs plan or wait until next month's reset (the free tier resets each month).

500+
ElevenLabs server error

Their service is having issues. Wait a few minutes and try again. Check status.elevenlabs.io.

Voice troubleshooting
Audio plays but the dropdown is empty

Your key has text_to_speech permission but not voices_read. ElevenLabs keys can be created with granular permissions — TTS without voice listing is one of the defaults. Recreate the key with both permissions enabled.

I added new voices on ElevenLabs but they don't show in YourBook

Click the "Natural" voice button — this re-fetches your voices from ElevenLabs. If they still don't appear, click the small refresh button (⟳) next to the dropdown.

The voice changes when I save a new API key

When you save a different key, the dropdown shows that account's voices. If your previously-selected voice doesn't exist on the new account, the app picks the first available one as default.

The natural voice sounds robotic or stuttering

This usually happens with very slow internet — the audio chunks aren't downloading fast enough. Pause and resume to reset, or switch back to the free Robotic voice.

PDF and chapter detection
Chapter list is wrong or missing

YourBook tries to detect chapters two ways: from the PDF's built-in outline, and from font-size/heading patterns in the text. If the PDF has a corrupted outline (every entry named the same thing) or unusual formatting, detection falls back to heuristics. You can still read the book — just navigate by clicking words.

The same PDF won't update with new chapter detection

Books are cached in your browser for speed. To force re-processing, delete the book from the Library page and re-upload it.

Long books load slowly or freeze

Books over 500 pages take time to process. Once processed, they're cached for instant loading next time. If your browser feels sluggish during a long book, that's the windowed-rendering mode kicking in to keep memory usage low.

Privacy

Everything stays in your browser. PDFs, highlights, notes, and your API key are stored locally in IndexedDB and localStorage — nothing is sent to any server except ElevenLabs (when you use the Natural voice) and only the text being read aloud.

Clearing your browser data will erase your library, highlights, and saved API key. There's no cloud sync.
